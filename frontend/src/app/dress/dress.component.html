Dress Code

For our upcoming summer evening celebration, we invite you to dress in stylish cocktail attire.

For Women:

A knee-length or midi dress in a light, airy fabric would be perfect. Feel free to choose bold colours, floral prints, or soft pastels to complement the summer theme. Pair with elegant heels or dressy sandals. However, a word of warning—the Distillery District has cobblestone walkways that may not be easy to navigate in heels!

For Men:

A suit or smart blazer with dress pants is ideal. You may opt for lighter shades to match the season. Pair with a dress shirt and a tie or bow tie for a polished look. You could even consider suspenders if you are really fashionable. Stylish loafers or dress shoes will complete the outfit.

We look forward to celebrating in style with you!
